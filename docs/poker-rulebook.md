# Poker — Rulebook

No-limit Texas Hold'em, as Khuzur plays it. Make the best 5-card hand from your 2 cards and the 5 shared on the table, or bet until everyone else folds.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Players** | 2 to 6 per table |
| **Game** | No-limit Texas Hold'em |
| **Free tables** | Everyone sits down with 1000 chips; blinds 5/10. The chips are free and count for nothing outside the table. |
| **Credit tables** | Played for credits that stay with your account (see [§9](#9-credit-tables)) |
| **Turn clock** | 20 seconds online; none in practice |

## 1. The goal

Win chips. Each hand you hold **2 cards of your own** and share **5 cards on the table**. The best 5-card hand made from those 7 wins the pot, or you win it by betting until everyone else folds.

---

## 2. A hand

1. **The button moves.** The dealer button moves one occupied seat left, skipping empty seats and players sitting out. The first hand's button is drawn at random, after a short countdown once the table starts.
2. **Blinds.** The next player posts the small blind (5), and the one after posts the big blind (10).
3. **The deal.** The house shuffles the deck on the felt and deals 2 cards to everyone dealt in, one at a time, starting left of the button. The deck then lies left of the board, and the flop, turn and river come from it.
4. **Preflop betting** starts left of the big blind.
5. **The board.** The flop (3 shared cards), then betting; the turn (1 card), then betting; the river (1 card), then betting. After the flop, betting starts at the first player left of the button.
6. **The showdown** (see [§5](#5-showdown-and-pots)).

> **Two players.** With two dealt in, the button posts the small blind and acts first preflop, and last after the flop.
>
> **A short blind.** A player who can't cover a blind posts everything they have and is all-in. The others still call the full big blind.

---

## 3. Betting

On your turn you can **fold**, **check** (when there's nothing to call), **call**, **bet** (when nobody has), **raise**, or go **all-in**.

- The first bet of a round is at least the big blind.
- A raise is at least the size of the last bet or raise in that round.
- An all-in can be any amount. An all-in short of a full raise doesn't reopen the betting: players who already acted may only call the extra or fold.
- The big blind has the option: if everyone just calls, they may still raise.
- A round ends when everyone still in has acted and matched the bet, or is all-in.

## 4. Hand ranks

Best first:

| # | Hand | Example |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Straight flush** (an ace-high one is a **royal flush**) | 9♠ 10♠ J♠ Q♠ K♠ |
| 2 | **Four of a kind** | 7♣ 7♦ 7♥ 7♠ |
| 3 | **Full house** | Q♣ Q♥ Q♠ 4♦ 4♠ |
| 4 | **Flush** | 2♥ 6♥ 9♥ J♥ A♥ |
| 5 | **Straight** | 5♣ 6♦ 7♠ 8♥ 9♣ |
| 6 | **Three of a kind** | 8♦ 8♥ 8♠ |
| 7 | **Two pair** | J♣ J♦ 3♥ 3♠ |
| 8 | **Pair** | A♦ A♠ |
| 9 | **High card** | — |

- A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest straight.
- Ties break on the remaining cards (kickers) within the best five. **Suits never break a tie.**

## 5. Showdown and pots

- **Everyone else folds:** the last player wins without showing.
- **Folded hands** go face down onto the muck, right of the board.
- **Uncalled bets:** a bet or raise that nobody calls, or the part of it nobody could match, goes back to the player who made it.
- **Who shows first.** At a river showdown, the last player to bet or raise on the river shows first. If the river was checked through, the first player left of the button does. A hand that wins nothing is mucked unshown.
- **All-in run-outs.** When betting can't continue (all but one player are all-in), every hand still in is shown, and the rest of the board is dealt with no more betting.
- **Side pots.** A player all-in for less can only win, from each opponent, as much as they put in themselves. The rest forms side pots among the players who put in more.
- **Split pots.** A tie splits the pot. Chips that don't divide evenly go one each to the tied winners, starting left of the button.

---

## 6. Seats

- **Sitting down.** Take any empty seat at any time. You're dealt in from the next hand.
- **Leaving.** Leave at any time. A hand you're in is folded, and no CPU takes your seat.
- **The turn clock.** Online, each turn has 20 seconds. When it runs out you check if you can, otherwise you fold. Time out twice in a row and you sit out: you keep your seat and chips, but aren't dealt in until you press **I'M BACK**. After five minutes sitting out, you leave the table.
- **Out of chips?** Press **REBUY** for another 1000. CPUs rebuy on their own.
- **The host** presses **START** once; hands then follow each other while two or more players have chips. At a free table the host can add CPUs (**Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard**), remove them, and close the table. Practice seats you with five CPUs and has no turn clock.

## 7. Chips

This round's bets sit in front of each player as stacks of chips, and collected bets form the pot in the middle, with side pots as tags beside it. The blinds' chips are marked **SB** and **BB**.

| Chip | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Cream | 1 |
| Rose | 5 |
| Green | 25 |
| Navy | 100 |
| Purple | 500 |
| Orange | 1000 |

A stack is drawn with the fewest chips that make its amount, biggest value first, one column per value. It shows at most 4 columns of 6 chips, so a big amount is drawn short; the number under every stack is exact.

## 8. Controls

| Key or button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **F** | Fold |
| **C** | Check or call |
| **R** | Raise by the amount set |
| **½ POT** / **POT** | Set a raise of half or all of the pot, after calling |
| **ALL-IN** | Set everything you have |
| Slider and amount box | Fine-tune the raise. **Enter** in the box raises. |

## 9. Credit tables

Signed-in players with a linked **Google** or **Discord** login can also play for **credits**, which stay with your account.

### Your balance

You start with **1000 credits**. Once every 24 hours, if your balance plus what you have at a table is under 1000, **CLAIM** tops you back up to exactly 1000. Everything above 1000 is winnings.

### Stakes

The host picks the stakes. Credit tables are for people only: no CPUs.

| Stakes | Blinds | Buy-in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LOW** | 5 / 10 | 200 – 1,000 |
| **MID** | 25 / 50 | 1,000 – 5,000 |
| **HIGH** | 100 / 200 | 4,000 – 20,000 |

### Playing

- **Buying in.** Sitting down moves the credits you choose from your balance to the table. If you left a table at the same stakes in the last hour, you must bring at least what you left with. You can sit at one credit table at a time.
- **The big blind.** A newcomer, or a player back from sitting out, is dealt in when the big blind reaches them, and posts it like everyone else.
- **Adding chips.** Between hands, or after you fold, **ADD CHIPS** tops your stack up to the table's maximum; the chips land before the next deal. Out of chips, **REBUY** works the same way from the table's minimum.
- **Leaving.** Your stack goes back to your balance. In a hand, your cards fold, unless you're all-in: then the hand plays out and whatever it wins goes to your balance.
- **Closing.** When the host closes the table, the hand in play finishes first, then everyone's credits go home. If the server restarts, every stack goes back to its balance and a hand that was being played doesn't count.
- **The leaderboard** ranks everyone who has played for credits by their balance plus what's at a table.
