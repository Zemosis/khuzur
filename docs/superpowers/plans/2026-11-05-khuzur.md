# Khuzur Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Khuzur as the hall's fifth game: practice tables against one CPU level, every table shape (free for all 2–6, 2v2, 2v2v2, 3v3) with the 5-card or 7-card deal, picked with the builder.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in `src/utils/khuzur/` (`cards.js` for card order, leads and covering; `engine.js` for the match state machine; `ai.js` for the CPU). They're written like Muushig's (state in, new state out, throw on illegal moves, `rng` argument) so they can be copied to `server/game/khuzur/` later. React pages (`LobbyKhuzur`, `GameKhuzur`) and components in `src/components/khuzur/` run practice entirely in the browser.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, react-router 7, Tailwind 4, gsap, vitest (projects `unit` and `ui`), @testing-library/react.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-khuzur-design.md`. **Rules:** `docs/khuzur-rulebook.md` is the source of truth; every engine rule cites its section.

## Global Constraints

- Engine and CPU files import nothing from React, the DOM or sockets; randomness only through an `rng` argument defaulting to `secureRandom` (copy Muushig's).
- Suit order, lowest to highest: `["♦", "♣", "♥", "♠"]`. Ranks: `["7","8","9","10","J","Q","K","A"]`.
- 5-card game: 34 cards (one of each, plus gray and colored joker). 7-card game: 66 cards (7–A twice, one gray and one colored joker).
- Lead sizes: 1, 3, 5, 7; never more than the hand size of the game (5 or 7) or the smallest hand still playing.
- Play and the deal go clockwise: the next seat is `seat + 1`. You sit at the bottom; other seats go clockwise from your left.
- Seats: `perTeam × teams ≤ 6`. Teams alternate seats: `team = seat % teams`.
- Practice only: no sockets, no credits, no XP, no `useSoloMatchReport`, no timer.
- No emoji or symbol glyphs in UI text (`docs/STYLEGUIDE.md` §3); suits on cards are fine. No rating or Elo anywhere.
- Every animation stops under reduced motion (`reducedMotion()` from `src/utils/motion.js`; CSS under `html[data-motion="reduced"]`).
- Commits: conventional, one line, no `Co-Authored-By` or any attribution trailer.
- Ships as `0.7.0` in `CHANGELOG.md` (format `## 0.7.0 — D Month YYYY`, player-facing notes).

## Review Focus

1. **Matches that never end.** Pick-ups can cycle cards between hands forever. Expected: every CPU-only match at every table shape finishes under a move cap (Task 5).
2. **A teammate going out removes the player whose turn it is.** In 2v2v2, when a team finishes, its other member must leave the stack's pending list, and the turn moves to the next pending player (Task 4).
3. **The pile runs dry partway through a draw.** A player who needs 3 cards takes the 1 left in the pile, then the face-up trump, and stops at 2 (Task 3).
4. **The smallest hand belongs to the leader, or to a player who is out.** The limit counts the leader's own hand and ignores players who are out (Task 2).
5. **Clicking during a CPU move or out of turn.** The engine throws; the page shows the reason and never crashes or double-applies a move (Task 8).

- Error messages (the page shows these as they are):

  - `"That isn't a valid lead"`
  - `"You can lead at most N cards"`
  - `"Those cards don't beat the top layer"`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** Use a `playState({ hands, trump, drawPile, trumpCard, turn, stack, table })` helper that builds a PLAY state directly.
  - **lead**
    - Puts the cards on a new stack.
    - `pending` holds every other non-out seat in clockwise order.
    - The leader draws back to `handSize`, and the turn moves to `pending[0]`.
    - It refuses invalid leads, leads over `leadLimit`, cards not in the hand, leading while a stack is open, and moves out of turn.
  - **beat**
    - The new layer is aligned by column, so `layers[1][i]` beats `layers[0][i]`.
    - The beater draws back up.
    - It refuses wrong counts and cards that don't beat.
    - Teammates must beat too: a 2v2 lead's `pending` includes the leader's teammate.
  - **Full circle**
    - In 4 players, when seats 1, 2 and 3 all beat seat 0's lead, the whole stack goes to `deadPile`, `stack` is `null`, and seat 3 leads.
    - In 1v1, one beat ends it and the beater leads.
  - **pickUp**
    - The picker's hand gains every card of every layer.
    - The turn goes to `nextIn(picker)`, and the picker doesn't draw.
    - A pick-up is allowed even when the player could beat.
    - It is refused from anyone but `pending[0]`.
  - **Drawing back up** (Review Focus 3)
    - With `drawPile` holding 1 card and the trump card on the table, a leader who played 3 of 5 takes the pile card, then the trump card, ending with 4.
    - `trumpCard` becomes `null`, `trumpSuit` is unchanged, and the next player draws nothing.
  - **swapTrumpSeven**
    - On your own turn, while leading or beating, it swaps your `7` of the trump suit with `trumpCard`.
    - It's refused when it isn't your turn, when the trump card has been drawn, and when `trumpCard` is itself a 7.
    - In the 7-card game, once one trump 7 has been swapped, the other trump 7 can't swap.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run.** Expected: the new tests FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** A shared `drawUp(state, seat)` takes from `drawPile`, then `trumpCard`, up to `handSize`. After every lead and beat, call `drawUp`, then `settleOut` (stubbed until Task 4 to only check for an empty hand), then advance:
  - after a lead, `turn = pending[0]`;
  - after a beat, `pending.shift()`; if `pending` is empty, the stack dies and `turn` is the beater if they're not out, else `nextIn(beater)`.

    - When a leader goes out by leading their last cards, the stack still needs every other non-out player.
  - **Free for all, 4 players:** places 1, 2, 3 are given as players go out, and the last player left gets place 4. The phase becomes MATCH_OVER.
  - **2v2 and 3v3:** the first player out makes their team place 1, the other team place 2, and the match ends.
  - **2v2v2** (Review Focus 2)
    - When seat 0 goes out while seat 3 (seat 0's teammate) is in `pending`, seat 3 is marked `out` and removed from `pending`.
    - The turn moves to the next pending seat, the team gets place 1, and the other two teams play on until one more team gets place 2. The last team gets place 3.

  - **surrender**
    - FFA: the surrendering seat gets the last place, and the remaining players are ranked by hand size, fewest first, ties by lower seat.
    - Teams: the whole team is last, and the other teams are ranked by their smallest member's hand.
    - Places already given are kept, and the phase becomes MATCH_OVER.
  - **startNextMatch**
    - FFA: the dealer is `nextIn(previous dealer)`, counting every seat because everyone is back in.
    - Teams: the last-place team deals. If `teamDealers[team]` exists, the dealer is that team's next member clockwise after it. Otherwise it's a random member, using `rng`.
    - Hands, piles and `places` reset, and nobody is `out`.

### Task 5: The CPU, and whole matches played to the end

**Files:**
- Create: `src/utils/khuzur/ai.js`
- Test: `tests/unit/khuzur-ai.test.js`, `tests/integration/khuzur-matches.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the engine (Tasks 1–4).
- Produces:
  - `aiAction(state, rng) → { type: "drawForDeal" | "lead" | "beat" | "pickUp" | "swapSeven", seat, cardIds?, depth? }` for `state.turn`. It returns `swapSeven` first when `canSwapSeven` is true.
  - `applyAction(state, action, rng) → state`, which runs the matching engine move.
  - `knownOut(state, seat) → Set<id>`: cards this seat can rule out of everyone else's hands (its own hand, `deadPile`, a `trumpCard` on the table).
  - `cheapestLead(hand, size, trumpSuit) → Card[] | null`: pairs from the lowest-power ranks first, plus the cheapest remaining card as the lonely card.
  - `cheapestCover(layer, hand, trumpSuit) → Card[] | null`: the lowest card that beats each layer card, taking layer cards from highest power down.

CPU policy (spec "The CPU"):
- **Lead**
  - If the whole hand is a valid lead within the limit, lead it (go out).
  - While the draw pile has cards, lead the largest `cheapestLead` with no trump or joker. Otherwise lead the lowest single.
  - Once the pile is dry, lead the largest size in `LEAD_SIZES` that's ≤ `leadLimit` and has a `cheapestLead`.
- **Beat**
  - Take `cheapestCover`. If it's `null`, pick up.
  - While the pile has cards, pick up instead when the cover uses a joker, or uses trumps and the stack has ≤ 3 cards.
  - Once the pile is dry, always beat when possible.
  - In team games, while the pile has cards, pick up rather than spend a trump on a stack whose top layer is a teammate's.
- **Draw for the deal:** a random depth from 1 to `min(MAX_DRAW_DEPTH, dealDeck.length)`.

  - `cheapestLead([9♣ 9♦ K♠ K♦ A♥ 7♣], 5, "♥")` is `9♣ 9♦ K♠ K♦ 7♣` (in any order).
  - `cheapestCover([9♣, K♠], [A♠ 10♣ 7♥], "♥")` uses `10♣` and `A♠`, not `7♥`.
  - The CPU goes out when its whole hand is a valid lead.
  - It swaps the trump 7 on its turn.
  - It picks up instead of covering a single `9♣` with its only coverer, `GJ`, while the pile has cards.
  - It never sees other hands: `aiAction` gives the same answer when every other player's hand is replaced with different cards of the same count.
  - Integration: play CPU-only matches through `aiAction`/`applyAction` with `seededRandom(seed)` for every valid table × both hand sizes, 20 seeds each. After every move, check:
    - every card of the deck is in exactly one place (hands, `drawPile`, `trumpCard`, `deadPile`, stack layers);
    - no hand of a player who isn't out is empty while the match is in PLAY.

    Every match must reach MATCH_OVER within 5000 moves (Review Focus 1). On failure, report the seed.
  - Strength: in 1v1, 5-card game, over 200 seeded matches against a random-legal-move bot, the CPU places 1st in at least 70%.
