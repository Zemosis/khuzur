# Credits Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Signed-in players get a credit balance (1000 to start, CLAIM back to 1000 once a day), play human-only credit poker tables in three tiers, climb an all-time leaderboard, and an admin can review and reverse suspicious transfers. No path can create or lose a credit.

**Architecture:** Every credit is in a balance (`credit_accounts`) or a seat (`credit_seats`), and every movement is a `credit_ledger` row. A `CreditTable` (`server/credits/tables.js`) wraps a credit table's `PokerTable` and runs every seat change (buy-in, adding chips, saving a finished hand, leaving, closing) through one queue per table. Each change is one database transaction, and the next hand doesn't deal until the last one is saved. The rules engine gains two settings: waiting for the big blind, and letting an all-in hand play out after its player leaves. It also gains one move, adding chips. Browsers reach credits through `/api/credits/*`, the admin through `/api/admin/*`, and the tables through the existing socket protocol.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22 + Express 5 + Socket.IO 4 + `pg` (server), Postgres 17, React 19 + Vite 7 + Tailwind 4 (site), Vitest 4 (unit / server / ui projects), supertest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-credits-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Tiers: LOW 5/10 buy-in 200–1000; MID 25/50 buy-in 1000–5000; HIGH 100/200 buy-in 4000–20000.
- Starting balance 1000; CLAIM tops up to exactly 1000, at most once per 24 hours from the last claim; the starting grant counts as the first claim.
- Rathole rule: for 1 hour after leaving a table at a tier, the minimum buy-in at that tier is the stack you left with, capped at the tier max.
- Credit play needs an account with a linked Google or Discord login (`oauth_identities` row), not frozen.
- One credit table per player at a time. Credit tables have 6 seats, no CPUs, no rake.
- Leaderboard: top 100 for everyone; others see their rank with 5 above and 5 below; recomputed at most every 30 seconds.
- Flags: 30-day window; A → B ≥ 1,000; ≥ 60% of A's losses; B → A ≤ 20% of A → B; big fold = folded after putting in ≥ half the stack.
- Every new table enables RLS (`server/tests/db/lockdown.test.js`). Never edit an applied migration.
- Rules files in `src/utils/poker/` are copied byte for byte to `server/game/poker/` (`tests/unit/rules-copies.test.js`): edit `src/`, then `cp`.
- The hall's pixel style (`docs/STYLEGUIDE.md`): Press Start 2P / VT323 fonts (`font-pixel-display`, `font-pixel-body`), hard 2–4px ink borders, no rounded corners or gradients on controls.
- Commits: conventional, one line, **no `Co-Authored-By` trailer** (the user's standing rule overrides any default). Commit on `main`; never push.
- Numbers shown to players use `fmt` (`2,340`).

## Review Focus

1. **Bad amounts over the socket**: a buy-in or ADD CHIPS of `"500"`, `500.5`, `-10`, `1e9`, `null` or `NaN` must be refused with a message, with no change to any balance or seat. (Task 3 `buyIn`/`topUp` tests; Task 6 socket test.)
2. **The browser closing while the buy-in is in flight**: the seat is taken, the 60s grace runs, and the credits come back. Nothing is left stuck in `credit_seats`. (Task 6 socket test.)
3. **The host closing the table while someone has added chips that haven't landed yet**: their stack plus those chips return to their balance. (Task 4 test.)
4. **The same account in two tabs**: one tab pressing LEAVE returns the credits exactly once, and the other tab can't keep playing the seat. (Task 6 socket test.)
5. **CLAIM while chips are in the pot**: a player with 100 in their balance and 900 all-in in a running hand holds 1,000 and can't claim. Ignoring the pot would let them claim 900. (Task 4 test, through `onTable`.)

## Changes from the spec found while planning

These were worked out while reading the code. Players don't see any of them:

- **No `hand_start_stack` column.** `credit_seats.stack` is always the stack at the last saved hand end, which is also the stack the running hand started from. A restart returns `stack + pending`.
- **One queue per credit table** (`CreditTable.enqueue`). Buy-in, adding chips, saving a hand, leaving and releasing run one at a time, so the database and the in-memory table never race.
- **`table_id` is a uuid per table run**, not the lobby code, which can repeat over time. `credit_hands` is unique on `(table_id, hand_number)`, so a save retried after a lost reply is a no-op.
- **Every save checks the money.** For each seated player, in-memory `stack + pending` must equal the database's `stack + pending` plus that hand's net. If not, the save throws and the table holds on "Saving…" rather than dealing on wrong numbers.
- **Leaving.** A player who leaves while in a hand, or between a hand's end and its save, is paid out inside that hand's save. Anyone else is paid out at once, from the database's `stack + pending`.
- **The engine allows adding chips at any time** (they wait in `pending`); "between hands or while folded" is enforced by the server and the UI.
- **A credit table always finishes its hand before closing.** That covers both the last member leaving and the host's CLOSE TABLE. Closing it straight away would void the hand and let a losing player escape it.
- **A busted player who rebuys waits for the big blind**, like a player back from sitting out.
- **Admin actions with no credits moved** (`freeze`, `unfreeze`, `dismiss`) are ledger rows with amount 0, so every admin action is in the ledger.
- **Hand summaries gain `hole`, `committed` and `board`.** The hole cards go to `credit_hands` only; they're stripped before `game_rounds`.
- **View field names follow the engine's.** A seat carries `waitingForBB`, and `leaving` on a seat still in the hand marks someone who left all-in. The spec called these `waitingForBigBlind` and `leftAllIn`.
- **The seat tag reads `WAITING FOR BB`**, which fits a phone seat plate. Your own area spells it out: "Waiting for the big blind to reach you".

```js
/**
 * The player leaves. In a hand, their cards fold now and the seat empties when
 * it ends — except an all-in at a table with allInPlaysOut: that hand plays
 * out, and whatever it wins is on the seat when it ends.
 */
export function standUp(state, seat) {
  const p = state.seats[seat];
  if (!p) return state;
  if (!handRunning(state) || !p.inHand) return withSeat(state, seat, null);
  if (state.allInPlaysOut && p.allIn && !p.folded) return patchSeat(state, seat, { leaving: true });
  let s = patchSeat(state, seat, { leaving: true });
  if (!p.folded) s = fold(s, seat);
  if (s.phase === PHASES.RUNOUT) return liveSeats(s).length === 1 ? winUncontested(s, liveSeats(s)[0]) : s;
  return settle(s, (s.turn + MAX_SEATS - 1) % MAX_SEATS);
}
```

```js
/**
 * Chips brought to a seat (credit tables): held as `pending` and put on the
 * stack when the next hand is dealt, so they never play in a hand already
 * running. Stack plus pending may not pass `max`. A busted player who adds
 * chips is back in, waiting for the big blind where the table asks for it.
 */
export function addChips(state, seat, amount, max = Infinity) {
  const p = state.seats[seat];
  if (!p) throw new Error("No one sits there");
  if (!Number.isInteger(amount) || amount <= 0) throw new Error("Pick an amount");
  if (p.stack + p.pending + amount > max) throw new Error(`You can have at most ${max} on the table`);
  const back = p.sittingOut && p.stack + p.pending === 0;
  return patchSeat(state, seat, {
    pending: p.pending + amount,
    ...(back ? { sittingOut: false, waitingForBB: !!state.waitForBigBlind } : {}),
  });
}
```

```js
/**
 * Who plays the next hand, and where the button and blinds go. Players
 * waiting for the big blind sit it out unless the big blind falls to them;
 * with fewer than two others there's nothing to wait for, so everyone ready
 * is dealt in.
 */
function seatsForHand(s, rng) {
  const ready = nextHandSeats(s);
  const settled = ready.filter((i) => !s.seats[i].waitingForBB);
  const waiting = s.button != null && settled.length >= 2 && settled.length < ready.length;
  const pool = waiting ? settled : ready;
  const inPool = (i) => pool.includes(i);
  const button = s.button == null ? pool[Math.floor(rng() * pool.length)] : nextFrom(s.button, inPool);
  if (waiting) {
    const sb = nextFrom(button, inPool);
    const bb = nextFrom(sb, (i) => ready.includes(i));
```

```js
// CREDIT TIERS — the stakes a credit table plays for and what you may bring
// to it. The browser's buy-in panel and the server's checks both read this
// file; server/game/poker/tiers.js is a byte-identical copy.

export const TIERS = {
  LOW: { id: "LOW", smallBlind: 5, bigBlind: 10, minBuyIn: 200, maxBuyIn: 1000 },
  MID: { id: "MID", smallBlind: 25, bigBlind: 50, minBuyIn: 1000, maxBuyIn: 5000 },
  HIGH: { id: "HIGH", smallBlind: 100, bigBlind: 200, minBuyIn: 4000, maxBuyIn: 20000 },
};
export const TIER_IDS = Object.keys(TIERS);
export const isTier = (id) => typeof id === "string" && Object.hasOwn(TIERS, id);

// Leave a table and, for this long, your next buy-in at that tier is at least the stack you left with.
export const RATHOLE_MS = 60 * 60 * 1000;

/** 2340 -> "2,340". */
export const fmt = (n) => Number(n).toLocaleString("en-US");

/**
 * What a player may sit down with at `tierId`: { min, max, ratholed, ok }.
 * rathole: { amount } — the stack they left a table at this tier with in the
 * last hour — or null.
 */
export function buyInRange(tierId, balance, rathole = null) {
  const t = TIERS[tierId];
  const ratholed = !!rathole && rathole.amount > t.minBuyIn;
  const min = ratholed ? Math.min(rathole.amount, t.maxBuyIn) : t.minBuyIn;
  const max = Math.min(t.maxBuyIn, balance);
  return { min, max, ratholed, ok: max >= min };
}

/**
 * What a seated player holding `onTable` (stack plus chips waiting to land)
 * may add: up to the tier max. With nothing left it's a rebuy, from the tier minimum.
 */
export function topUpRange(tierId, balance, onTable) {
  const t = TIERS[tierId];
  const min = onTable === 0 ? t.minBuyIn : 1;
  const max = Math.max(0, Math.min(t.maxBuyIn - onTable, balance));
  return { min, max, ok: max >= min };
}
```

```sql
  balance bigint not null check (balance >= 0),
  last_claim_at timestamptz not null default now(), -- the starting grant counts as a claim
  first_hand_at timestamptz,                        -- on the leaderboard from their first credit hand
  frozen_at timestamptz,
  frozen_note text,
  restart_notice jsonb,                             -- { amount, tableName }, shown once after a restart
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);

-- The credits at tables right now: one seat per player. `stack` is the stack
-- at the last saved hand (so also what a running hand started from);
-- `pending` is chips added since, landing at the next deal.
create table credit_seats (
  user_id uuid primary key references credit_accounts (user_id) on delete cascade,
  table_id uuid not null,
  table_name text not null,
  tier credit_tier not null,
  stack bigint not null check (stack >= 0),
  pending bigint not null default 0 check (pending >= 0),
  seated_at timestamptz not null default now()
);
create index credit_seats_table_id_idx on credit_seats (table_id);

-- Every finished credit hand. `seats` holds each player's result and hole
-- cards; only the server and the admin page read it.
create table credit_hands (
  id bigserial primary key,
  table_id uuid not null,
  session_id uuid references game_sessions (id) on delete set null,
  tier credit_tier not null,
  hand_number integer not null,
  is_private boolean not null default false,
  seats jsonb not null,
  board jsonb not null default '[]',
  events jsonb not null default '[]',
  played_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  unique (table_id, hand_number)
);
create index credit_hands_played_at_idx on credit_hands (played_at);
create index credit_hands_seats_idx on credit_hands using gin (seats jsonb_path_ops);

create table credit_ledger (
  id bigserial primary key,
  user_id uuid not null references users (id) on delete cascade,
  kind credit_kind not null,
  amount bigint not null, -- signed, as the balance sees it
  balance_after bigint not null,
  tier credit_tier,
  table_id uuid,
  hand_id bigint references credit_hands (id) on delete set null,
  note text,
  actor_id uuid references users (id) on delete set null, -- the admin, for admin kinds
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);
create index credit_ledger_user_id_created_at_idx on credit_ledger (user_id, created_at);
create index credit_ledger_kind_created_at_idx on credit_ledger (kind, created_at);

-- A flagged pair an admin looked at: hidden until new credits flow between them.
create table credit_flag_reviews (
  loser_id uuid not null references users (id) on delete cascade,
  winner_id uuid not null references users (id) on delete cascade,
  reviewed_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  reviewed_by uuid references users (id) on delete set null,
  note text not null,
  primary key (loser_id, winner_id)
);

-- Hidden from Supabase's Data API like every other table (003_lock_down.sql).
alter table credit_accounts enable row level security;
alter table credit_seats enable row level security;
alter table credit_hands enable row level security;
alter table credit_ledger enable row level security;
alter table credit_flag_reviews enable row level security;

-- A poker session's stakes: null for free chips.
alter table game_sessions add column poker_tier credit_tier;

-- Poker stats per player and stakes, so the profile can show free and credit play apart.
drop view player_poker_stats;
create view player_poker_stats as
select
  gp.player_id,
  case when gs.poker_tier is null then 'free' else 'credits' end    as stakes,
  count(*)                                                          as sessions,
  coalesce(sum((gp.stats ->> 'hands_played')::int), 0)              as hands_played,
  coalesce(sum((gp.stats ->> 'hands_won')::int), 0)                 as hands_won,
  coalesce(sum(gp.final_score), 0)                                  as net,
  coalesce(max((gp.stats ->> 'biggest_pot')::int), 0)               as biggest_pot,
  coalesce(sum((gp.stats ->> 'vpip_hands')::int), 0)                as vpip_hands,
  coalesce(sum((gp.stats ->> 'showdowns')::int), 0)                 as showdowns,
  coalesce(sum((gp.stats ->> 'showdowns_won')::int), 0)             as showdowns_won,
  coalesce(sum(extract(epoch from (coalesce(gp.left_at, gs.finished_at) - coalesce(gp.joined_at, gs.started_at)))::integer), 0) as total_seconds,
  max(gs.finished_at)                                               as last_played_at
from game_players gp
join game_sessions gs on gs.id = gp.session_id
where gs.game_type = 'poker'
  and gs.status = 'finished'
  and gp.player_id is not null
group by gp.player_id, stakes;
```

- [ ] **Step 4: Implement `server/credits/rules.js`**

```js
// CREDIT RULES — the numbers and checks the credit code shares, free of the
// database so they can be tested alone.

export const START_BALANCE = 1000;
export const CLAIM_TO = 1000;
export const CLAIM_EVERY_MS = 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000;
const RETRY_MS = Number(process.env.CREDIT_RETRY_MS) || 5000;

/** A refusal the player can act on: its message is shown to them as is. */
export class CreditError extends Error {}

/**
 * Where CLAIM stands: { state: "ready", amount } | { state: "wait", nextAt }
 * | { state: "over" } | { state: "frozen" }. onTable: what the player holds
 * at a credit table — stack, chips waiting to land and chips in the pot.
 */
export function claimState({ balance, lastClaimAt, frozen = false, onTable = 0, now = Date.now() }) {
  if (frozen) return { state: "frozen" };
  const total = balance + onTable;
  if (total >= CLAIM_TO) return { state: "over" };
  const nextAt = new Date(lastClaimAt).getTime() + CLAIM_EVERY_MS;
  if (now < nextAt) return { state: "wait", nextAt: new Date(nextAt).toISOString() };
  return { state: "ready", amount: CLAIM_TO - total };
}

/** 51_600_000 -> "14h 20m" (rounded up to the minute). */
export function untilText(ms) {
  const minutes = Math.ceil(ms / 60_000);
  return `${Math.floor(minutes / 60)}h ${minutes % 60}m`;
}

/** { ok: false, error } for a refusal; anything else is logged and told as "unavailable". */
export function failure(err) {
  if (err instanceof CreditError) return { ok: false, error: err.message };
  console.error("[credits]", err);
  return { ok: false, error: "Credits are unavailable right now. Try again in a moment." };
}

/** Runs fn until it succeeds (the database may be briefly away). Refusals aren't retried. */
export async function retry(fn, label) {
  for (;;) {
    try {
      return await fn();
    } catch (err) {
      if (err instanceof CreditError) throw err;
      console.error(`[credits] ${label} failed, retrying:`, err.message);
      await new Promise((resolve) => setTimeout(resolve, RETRY_MS));
    }
  }
}
```

```js
          if (!(err instanceof CreditError)) throw err;
        }
        if (op % 50 === 0) expect((await C.economyCheck()).ok).toBe(true);
      }
      expect((await C.economyCheck()).ok).toBe(true);
```

```js
// CREDIT ACCOUNTS — balances, CLAIM, and credits moving between a balance and
// a credit table seat. Each change is one transaction that locks the account
// row and writes a credit_ledger row, so two requests at once can't spend the
// same credits twice and every credit's history is on record.

import { pool, withTransaction } from "../db/index.js";
import { TIERS, TIER_IDS, RATHOLE_MS, buyInRange, topUpRange, fmt } from "../game/poker/tiers.js";
import { CreditError, START_BALANCE, claimState, untilText } from "./rules.js";

const num = (v) => (v == null ? null : Number(v));

/** The account row, locked for this transaction; opened (with its starting grant) on first touch. */
export async function lockAccount(client, userId) {
  const made = await client.query(
    "insert into credit_accounts (user_id, balance) values ($1, $2) on conflict (user_id) do nothing returning user_id",
    [userId, START_BALANCE],
  );
  if (made.rowCount) await ledger(client, { userId, kind: "grant", amount: START_BALANCE, balanceAfter: START_BALANCE });
  const {
    rows: [a],
  } = await client.query("select * from credit_accounts where user_id = $1 for update", [userId]);
  return { ...a, balance: num(a.balance) };
}

export async function ledger(client, { userId, kind, amount, balanceAfter, tier = null, tableId = null, handId = null, note = null, actorId = null }) {
  await client.query(
    `insert into credit_ledger (user_id, kind, amount, balance_after, tier, table_id, hand_id, note, actor_id)
     values ($1, $2, $3, $4, $5, $6, $7, $8, $9)`,
    [userId, kind, amount, balanceAfter, tier, tableId, handId, note, actorId],
  );
}

export async function setBalance(client, userId, balance) {
  await client.query("update credit_accounts set balance = $2 where user_id = $1", [userId, balance]);
}

/** Why this account can't play for credits, or null. */
async function whyCantPlay(client, userId, account) {
  if (account.frozen_at) return "Credit play is suspended on this account";
  const {
    rows: [r],
  } = await client.query("select exists (select 1 from oauth_identities where user_id = $1) as linked", [userId]);
  return r.linked ? null : "Link Google or Discord to play for credits";
}

/** The stack this player left a `tier` table with in the last hour: { amount, until } or null. */
async function ratholeFor(client, userId, tier) {
  const {
    rows: [r],
  } = await client.query(
    `select amount, created_at from credit_ledger
      where user_id = $1 and kind = 'cash_out' and tier = $2 and created_at > now() - make_interval(secs => $3)
      order by id desc limit 1`,
    [userId, tier, RATHOLE_MS / 1000],
  );
  return r ? { amount: num(r.amount), until: new Date(r.created_at.getTime() + RATHOLE_MS).toISOString() } : null;
}

/** A seat's credits back to the balance, the seat gone. */
async function payOut(client, userId, tableId, { tier, amount, kind = "cash_out", handId = null }) {
  const a = await lockAccount(client, userId);
  const balance = a.balance + amount;
  await setBalance(client, userId, balance);
  await client.query("delete from credit_seats where user_id = $1 and table_id = $2", [userId, tableId]);
  await ledger(client, { userId, kind, amount, balanceAfter: balance, tier, tableId, handId });
  return balance;
}

/**
 * Everything the CREDITS tab shows. onTable: what the player holds at the
 * table in memory right now (null: read the saved seat). A restart notice is
 * handed over once.
 */
export function me(userId, onTable = null) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const a = await lockAccount(client, userId);
    const whyNot = await whyCantPlay(client, userId, a);
    const {
      rows: [seat],
    } = await client.query("select table_name, tier, stack, pending from credit_seats where user_id = $1", [userId]);
    const held = onTable ?? (seat ? num(seat.stack) + num(seat.pending) : 0);
    const rathole = {};
    for (const tier of TIER_IDS) rathole[tier] = await ratholeFor(client, userId, tier);
    if (a.restart_notice) await client.query("update credit_accounts set restart_notice = null where user_id = $1", [userId]);
    return {
      balance: a.balance,
      onTable: held,
      canPlay: !whyNot,
      whyNot,
      claim: claimState({ balance: a.balance, lastClaimAt: a.last_claim_at, frozen: !!a.frozen_at, onTable: held }),
      seat: seat ? { tableName: seat.table_name, tier: seat.tier } : null,
      rathole,
      notice: a.restart_notice ?? null,
    };
  });
}

/** CLAIM: back up to 1000, once a day, while balance plus table is under it. */
export function claim(userId, onTable = 0) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const a = await lockAccount(client, userId);
    const whyNot = await whyCantPlay(client, userId, a);
    if (whyNot) throw new CreditError(whyNot);
    const state = claimState({ balance: a.balance, lastClaimAt: a.last_claim_at, frozen: !!a.frozen_at, onTable });
    if (state.state === "over") throw new CreditError("You have 1,000 or more: nothing to claim");
    if (state.state === "wait") throw new CreditError(`Your next claim is in ${untilText(Date.parse(state.nextAt) - Date.now())}`);
    const balance = a.balance + state.amount;
    await client.query("update credit_accounts set balance = $2, last_claim_at = now() where user_id = $1", [userId, balance]);
    await ledger(client, { userId, kind: "claim", amount: state.amount, balanceAfter: balance });
    return { balance };
  });
}

/** Sitting down: `amount` from the balance to a new seat at `tableId`. */
export function buyIn(userId, { tableId, tableName, tier, amount }) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const a = await lockAccount(client, userId);
    const whyNot = await whyCantPlay(client, userId, a);
    if (whyNot) throw new CreditError(whyNot);
    const {
      rows: [seated],
    } = await client.query("select table_name from credit_seats where user_id = $1", [userId]);
    if (seated) throw new CreditError(`You're already at table ${seated.table_name}`);
    if (!Number.isInteger(amount)) throw new CreditError("Pick an amount");
    const rathole = await ratholeFor(client, userId, tier);
    const range = buyInRange(tier, a.balance, rathole);
    const max = TIERS[tier].maxBuyIn;
    if (amount < range.min) {
      throw new CreditError(
        range.ratholed
          ? `You left a ${tier} table with ${fmt(rathole.amount)} within the hour: buy in with at least ${fmt(range.min)}`
          : `This table's minimum is ${fmt(range.min)}`,
      );
    }
    if (amount > max) throw new CreditError(`This table's maximum is ${fmt(max)}`);
    if (amount > a.balance) throw new CreditError(`Your balance is ${fmt(a.balance)}`);
    const balance = a.balance - amount;
    await setBalance(client, userId, balance);
    await client.query("insert into credit_seats (user_id, table_id, table_name, tier, stack) values ($1, $2, $3, $4, $5)", [userId, tableId, tableName, tier, amount]);
    await ledger(client, { userId, kind: "buy_in", amount: -amount, balanceAfter: balance, tier, tableId });
    return { balance };
  });
}

/** ADD CHIPS / REBUY: from the balance into the seat's pending. onTable: its stack plus pending now. */
export function topUp(userId, amount, { tier, tableId, onTable }) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const a = await lockAccount(client, userId);
    if (a.frozen_at) throw new CreditError("Credit play is suspended on this account");
    if (!Number.isInteger(amount) || amount < 1) throw new CreditError("Pick an amount");
    const range = topUpRange(tier, a.balance, onTable);
    const max = TIERS[tier].maxBuyIn;
    if (amount < range.min) throw new CreditError(`Buy back in with at least ${fmt(range.min)}`);
    if (onTable + amount > max) throw new CreditError(`You can have at most ${fmt(max)} on this table`);
    if (amount > a.balance) throw new CreditError(`Your balance is ${fmt(a.balance)}`);
    const updated = await client.query("update credit_seats set pending = pending + $3 where user_id = $1 and table_id = $2", [userId, tableId, amount]);
    if (!updated.rowCount) throw new CreditError("You're not at this table");
    const balance = a.balance - amount;
    await setBalance(client, userId, balance);
    await ledger(client, { userId, kind: "top_up", amount: -amount, balanceAfter: balance, tier, tableId });
    return { balance };
  });
}

/** Leaving between hands: the saved stack plus pending back to the balance. Safe to repeat. */
export function cashOut(userId, tableId) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const {
      rows: [seat],
    } = await client.query("select tier, stack, pending from credit_seats where user_id = $1 and table_id = $2 for update", [userId, tableId]);
    if (!seat) return { paid: 0 };
    const paid = num(seat.stack) + num(seat.pending);
    await payOut(client, userId, tableId, { tier: seat.tier, amount: paid });
    return { paid };
  });
}

/**
 * A finished hand: its record, every seat's new stack, and leavers paid out,
 * in one transaction. seats: everyone at the table in memory now
 * ({ userId, stack, pending, leaving }). Each must hold what the database
 * says plus what the hand won or lost them, or nothing is written. Returns
 * the hand's id, or null if it was saved already (a retry after a lost reply).
 */
export function saveHand({ tableId, tier, isPrivate, sessionId, summary, events, seats }) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const seatsJson = summary.seatResults.map((r) => ({
      seat: r.seat_index,
      user_id: r.player_key,
      stack_before: r.stack_before,
      stack_after: r.stack_after,
      net: r.net,
      won: r.won,
      committed: r.committed,
      folded_street: r.folded_street,
      shown_hand: r.shown_hand,
      hole: r.hole,
    }));
    const inserted = await client.query(
      `insert into credit_hands (table_id, session_id, tier, hand_number, is_private, seats, board, events)
       values ($1, $2, $3, $4, $5, $6, $7, $8)
       on conflict (table_id, hand_number) do nothing returning id`,
      [tableId, sessionId, tier, summary.roundNumber, !!isPrivate, JSON.stringify(seatsJson), JSON.stringify(summary.board ?? []), JSON.stringify(events ?? [])],
    );
    if (!inserted.rowCount) return null;
    const handId = inserted.rows[0].id;

    const { rows } = await client.query(
      "select user_id, stack, pending from credit_seats where table_id = $1 and user_id = any($2::uuid[]) for update",
      [tableId, seats.map((x) => x.userId)],
    );
    const saved = new Map(rows.map((r) => [r.user_id, num(r.stack) + num(r.pending)]));
    const net = new Map(summary.seatResults.map((r) => [r.player_key, r.net]));
    for (const x of seats) {
      const expected = saved.has(x.userId) ? saved.get(x.userId) + (net.get(x.userId) ?? 0) : null;
      if (x.stack + x.pending !== expected) {
        throw new Error(`credits at table ${tableId} don't match the database for ${x.userId}: ${x.stack + x.pending} vs ${expected}`);
      }
    }
    for (const x of seats) {
      if (x.leaving) await payOut(client, x.userId, tableId, { tier, amount: x.stack + x.pending, handId });
      else await client.query("update credit_seats set stack = $3, pending = $4 where user_id = $1 and table_id = $2", [x.userId, tableId, x.stack, x.pending]);
    }
    const dealt = summary.seatResults.map((r) => r.player_key).filter(Boolean);
    await client.query("update credit_accounts set first_hand_at = coalesce(first_hand_at, now()) where user_id = any($1::uuid[])", [dealt]);
    return handId;
  });
}

/** Every seat at `tableId` home at its saved stack plus pending (a hand still running is void). */
export function releaseTable(tableId, kind = "cash_out") {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const { rows } = await client.query("select user_id, tier, stack, pending from credit_seats where table_id = $1 order by user_id for update", [tableId]);
    const paid = [];
    for (const r of rows) {
      const amount = num(r.stack) + num(r.pending);
      await payOut(client, r.user_id, tableId, { tier: r.tier, amount, kind });
      paid.push({ userId: r.user_id, amount });
    }
    return paid;
  });
}

/** At boot: no table survived, so every seat goes home, and each player is told once. Returns how many. */
export function returnAllSeats() {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const { rows } = await client.query("select user_id, table_id, table_name, tier, stack, pending from credit_seats order by user_id for update");
    for (const r of rows) {
      const amount = num(r.stack) + num(r.pending);
      await payOut(client, r.user_id, r.table_id, { tier: r.tier, amount, kind: "restart_return" });
      await client.query("update credit_accounts set restart_notice = $2 where user_id = $1", [r.user_id, JSON.stringify({ amount, tableName: r.table_name })]);
    }
    return rows.length;
  });
}
```

```js
// CREDIT TABLES — a credit table's PokerTable plus the credits behind it.
// Every change to who holds what goes through one queue per table, so the
// table in memory and the database never disagree: buying in, adding chips,
// saving each finished hand (the next deal waits for it), leaving, and
// releasing everyone's credits when the table closes.

import { randomUUID } from "node:crypto";
import { PokerTable } from "../game/poker/table.js";
import { TIERS } from "../game/poker/tiers.js";
import * as accounts from "./accounts.js";
import { failure, retry } from "./rules.js";

const running = (s) => s.phase === "BETTING" || s.phase === "RUNOUT";

export class CreditTable {
  /**
   * name, tier ("LOW" | "MID" | "HIGH"), isPrivate; sessionId() → a promise of
   * the table's game_sessions id (or null). Everything else (delays, onState,
   * onHandEnd, onKick, onClosed, rng) goes to the PokerTable. `store` is the
   * accounts module; tests swap it.
   */
  constructor({ name, tier, isPrivate = false, sessionId = async () => null, store = accounts, ...options }) {
    const t = TIERS[tier];
    this.id = randomUUID(); // this run of the table: lobby codes repeat over time, this never does
    this.name = name;
    this.tier = tier;
    this.isPrivate = isPrivate;
    this.sessionId = sessionId;
    this.store = store;
    this.queue = Promise.resolve();
    this.unsaved = false; // a hand has ended and its save hasn't finished
    this.leavers = new Map(); // userId -> [resolve]: paid out by the hand being played or saved
    this.released = false;
    this.table = new PokerTable({
      ...options,
      smallBlind: t.smallBlind,
      bigBlind: t.bigBlind,
      buyIn: t.minBuyIn,
      waitForBigBlind: true,
      allInPlaysOut: true,
      info: { tier },
      beforeNextHand: (summary) => this.saveHand(summary),
    });
  }

  /** Runs `job` after every job queued before it. Its result (or error) is the caller's. */
  enqueue(job) {
    const run = this.queue.then(job);
    this.queue = run.catch(() => {});
    return run;
  }

  seatOf(userId) {
    return this.table.state.seats.findIndex((p) => p?.type === "HUMAN" && p.key === userId);
  }

  /** What this player holds here — stack, chips waiting to land, chips in the pot — or null. */
  onTable(userId) {
    const p = this.table.state.seats[this.seatOf(userId)];
    if (!p) return null;
    return p.stack + p.pending + (running(this.table.state) ? p.committed : 0);
  }

  /**
   * Buys in with `amount` and sits in the first empty seat. onSeat(seat) runs
   * just before the table announces them. → { ok, seat } | { ok: false, error }.
   */
  sit({ userId, name, avatar = null }, amount, onSeat = () => {}) {
    return this.enqueue(async () => {
      if (this.released) return { ok: false, error: "This table is closing" };
      const seat = this.table.firstFreeSeat();
      if (seat === -1) return { ok: false, error: "The table is full" };
      try {
        await this.store.buyIn(userId, { tableId: this.id, tableName: this.name, tier: this.tier, amount });
      } catch (err) {
        return failure(err);
      }
      onSeat(seat);
      const result = this.table.sit({ name, type: "HUMAN", avatar, key: userId, stack: amount }, seat);
      // The seat was checked inside the queue, so this can't fail; if it ever does, the credits go back.
      if (!result.ok) await retry(() => this.store.cashOut(userId, this.id), `refunding ${userId}`);
      return result;
    });
  }

  /** ADD CHIPS / REBUY: credits from the balance onto the seat, landing before the next deal. */
  addChips(userId, amount) {
    return this.enqueue(async () => {
      const seat = this.seatOf(userId);
      const p = this.table.state.seats[seat];
      if (!p || p.leaving || this.leavers.has(userId)) return { ok: false, error: "You're not at this table" };
      if (running(this.table.state) && p.inHand && !p.folded) return { ok: false, error: "Add chips between hands" };
      try {
        await this.store.topUp(userId, amount, { tier: this.tier, tableId: this.id, onTable: p.stack + p.pending });
      } catch (err) {
        return failure(err);
      }
      // The database has the credits: they land whatever the table did meanwhile.
      return this.table.addChips(this.seatOf(userId), amount);
    });
  }

  /**
   * The player leaves. Out of any hand their credits go back now; in one (or
   * while the hand that just ended is saved) that hand's save pays them.
   * Resolves once the credits are back in their balance.
   */
  leave(userId) {
    let paid;
    const done = new Promise((resolve) => (paid = resolve));
    this.enqueue(async () => {
      const seat = this.seatOf(userId);
      const p = this.table.state.seats[seat];
      if (!p) return paid();
      if (this.leavers.has(userId)) return this.leavers.get(userId).push(paid);
      const inHand = running(this.table.state) && p.inHand;
      if (inHand || this.unsaved) {
        this.leavers.set(userId, [paid]);
        if (inHand) this.table.standUp(seat); // folds, or an all-in plays out; the seat empties after the save
        return;
      }
      this.table.standUp(seat);
      await retry(() => this.store.cashOut(userId, this.id), `paying out ${userId}`);
      paid();
    });
    return done;
  }

  /** beforeNextHand: the hand that just ended and everyone's credits, in one transaction. */
  saveHand(summary) {
    this.unsaved = true;
    return this.enqueue(async () => {
      if (this.released) return; // everyone went home at the stacks last saved
      const s = this.table.state;
      const seats = s.seats.flatMap((p, seat) =>
        p?.type === "HUMAN" ? [{ seat, userId: p.key, stack: p.stack, pending: p.pending, leaving: p.leaving || this.leavers.has(p.key) }] : [],
      );
      await this.store.saveHand({ tableId: this.id, tier: this.tier, isPrivate: this.isPrivate, sessionId: await this.sessionId(), summary, events: s.events, seats });
      this.unsaved = false;
      for (const x of seats.filter((x) => x.leaving)) {
        if (this.table.state.seats[x.seat]) this.table.standUp(x.seat); // the hand is over: the seat empties now
        for (const resolve of this.leavers.get(x.userId) ?? []) resolve();
        this.leavers.delete(x.userId);
      }
    });
  }

  /** Everyone's credits home at the stacks last saved; a hand still running is void. */
  release(kind = "cash_out") {
    return this.enqueue(async () => {
      this.released = true;
      await retry(() => this.store.releaseTable(this.id, kind), `releasing table ${this.id}`);
      for (const list of this.leavers.values()) for (const resolve of list) resolve();
      this.leavers.clear();
    });
  }

  destroy() {
    this.table.destroy();
  }
}

/** At boot: no table survived the restart, so every seat's credits go home. */
export async function returnSeatsAfterRestart() {
  const n = await accounts.returnAllSeats();
  if (n) console.log(`[credits] returned ${n} seat(s) to their balances after a restart`);
  return n;
}
```

```js
// LEADERBOARD — everyone who has played a credit hand, by balance plus what
// they hold at a table (as last saved), worked out at most every 30 seconds.
// Frozen accounts are left out. Rows never carry user ids.

import { pool } from "../db/index.js";

export const TOP = 100;
const AROUND = 5;
const CACHE_MS = 30_000;
let cache = { at: 0, rows: null };

export function clearLeaderboardCache() {
  cache = { at: 0, rows: null };
}

async function ranked() {
  if (cache.rows && Date.now() - cache.at < CACHE_MS) return cache.rows;
  const { rows } = await pool.query(`
    select a.user_id, p.username, p.tag, p.avatar, p.custom_avatar,
           a.balance + coalesce(s.stack + s.pending, 0)                                as total,
           s.user_id is not null                                                       as at_table,
           rank() over (order by a.balance + coalesce(s.stack + s.pending, 0) desc)    as rank
      from credit_accounts a
      join profiles p on p.id = a.user_id
      left join credit_seats s on s.user_id = a.user_id
     where a.frozen_at is null and a.first_hand_at is not null and p.username is not null
     order by total desc, lower(p.username), p.tag`);
  cache = {
    at: Date.now(),
    rows: rows.map((r) => ({
      userId: r.user_id,
      rank: Number(r.rank),
      name: r.username,
      tag: r.tag,
      avatar: { variant: r.avatar, custom: r.avatar === "custom" ? r.custom_avatar : null },
      total: Number(r.total),
      atTable: r.at_table,
    })),
  };
  return cache.rows;
}

const shown = ({ userId, ...row }, me) => ({ ...row, me: userId === me });

/** { top: the first 100, me: { rank, total, around } | null }. around: the 5 above and below, only outside the top 100. */
export async function leaderboard(userId = null) {
  const rows = await ranked();
  const i = userId ? rows.findIndex((r) => r.userId === userId) : -1;
  return {
    top: rows.slice(0, TOP).map((r) => shown(r, userId)),
    me:
      i === -1
        ? null
        : { rank: rows[i].rank, total: rows[i].total, around: i < TOP ? null : rows.slice(i - AROUND, i + AROUND + 1).map((r) => shown(r, userId)) },
  };
}

export async function rankOf(userId) {
  return (await ranked()).find((r) => r.userId === userId)?.rank ?? null;
}
```

```js
// CREDITS ROUTES — /api/credits: your balance and CLAIM, and the leaderboard.

import express from "express";
import { optionalUser, requireDb, requireUser } from "../auth.js";
import * as accounts from "./accounts.js";
import { leaderboard, rankOf } from "./leaderboard.js";
import { CreditError } from "./rules.js";

/** onTableFor(userId): what that player holds at a credit table right now (stack, chips waiting, chips in the pot), or null. */
export function creditsRouter({ onTableFor = () => null } = {}) {
  const router = express.Router();
  router.use(express.json({ limit: "4kb" }), requireDb);
  const handle = (fn) => async (req, res) => {
    try {
      res.json(await fn(req));
    } catch (err) {
      if (err instanceof CreditError) return res.status(400).json({ error: err.message });
      console.error("[credits]", err);
      res.status(500).json({ error: "Credits are unavailable right now" });
    }
  };
  const meFor = async (userId) => ({ ...(await accounts.me(userId, onTableFor(userId))), rank: await rankOf(userId) });

  router.get("/me", requireUser, handle((req) => meFor(req.user.id)));
  router.post(
    "/claim",
    requireUser,
    handle(async (req) => {
      await accounts.claim(req.user.id, onTableFor(req.user.id) ?? 0);
      return meFor(req.user.id);
    }),
  );
  router.get("/leaderboard", optionalUser, handle((req) => leaderboard(req.user?.id ?? null)));
  return router;
}
```

```js
/** Sat out too long: off the table, out of its chat, back to the lobby. */
function kickFromPoker(lobby, seat) {
  const member = [...lobby.members.values()].find((m) => m.seatIndex === seat);
  if (!member) {
    const key = lobby.game.state.seats[seat]?.key;
    return lobby.credits ? lobby.credits.leave(key) : lobby.game.standUp(seat);
  }
  if (member.connected && member.socketId) io.to(member.socketId).emit("table_left", { reason: "away" });
  io.sockets.sockets.get(member.socketId)?.leave(lobby.id);
  removeMember(lobby, member);
}
```

```js
// FLAG RULES — which pairs of accounts look like one feeding the other
// credits. Pure: flags.js reads the hands, this decides.

export const FLAG_WINDOW_DAYS = 30;
export const FLAG_MIN_FLOW = 1000; // A lost at least this much to B...
export const FLAG_MIN_SHARE = 0.6; // ...which is at least this share of everything A lost...
export const FLAG_MAX_BACKFLOW = 0.2; // ...and B lost back at most this share of it.
export const BIG_FOLD_SHARE = 0.5; // a clue: folding after putting in half your stack or more

/**
 * hands: [{ id, playedAt, isPrivate, seats: [{ user_id, net, stack_before, committed, folded_street }] }].
 * Each hand's losses are shared among its winners in proportion to what they won.
 * → { pairs: Map("loser>winner" → { loser, winner, flow, hands, privateHands, bigFolds, lastAt }),
 *     lost: Map(loser → everything they lost) }
 */
export function pairFlows(hands) {
  const pairs = new Map();
  const lost = new Map();
  for (const h of hands) {
    const winners = h.seats.filter((s) => s.user_id && s.net > 0);
    const won = winners.reduce((t, s) => t + s.net, 0);
    for (const l of h.seats.filter((s) => s.user_id && s.net < 0)) {
      lost.set(l.user_id, (lost.get(l.user_id) ?? 0) - l.net);
      const bigFold = l.folded_street != null && l.committed >= l.stack_before * BIG_FOLD_SHARE;
      for (const w of winners) {
        const key = `${l.user_id}>${w.user_id}`;
        const p = pairs.get(key) ?? { loser: l.user_id, winner: w.user_id, flow: 0, hands: 0, privateHands: 0, bigFolds: 0, lastAt: null };
        p.flow += (-l.net * w.net) / won;
        p.hands += 1;
        if (h.isPrivate) p.privateHands += 1;
        if (bigFold) p.bigFolds += 1;
        if (!p.lastAt || h.playedAt > p.lastAt) p.lastAt = h.playedAt;
        pairs.set(key, p);
      }
    }
  }
  return { pairs, lost };
}

/** The pairs to flag, newest flow first. reviews: Map("loser>winner" → when an admin dismissed it). */
export function flagPairs({ pairs, lost }, reviews = new Map()) {
  const flags = [];
  for (const [key, p] of pairs) {
    const back = pairs.get(`${p.winner}>${p.loser}`)?.flow ?? 0;
    const share = p.flow / lost.get(p.loser);
    const reviewed = reviews.get(key);
    if (p.flow < FLAG_MIN_FLOW || share < FLAG_MIN_SHARE || back > p.flow * FLAG_MAX_BACKFLOW) continue;
    if (reviewed && reviewed >= p.lastAt) continue;
    flags.push({ ...p, flow: Math.round(p.flow), back: Math.round(back), share: Math.round(share * 100) / 100 });
  }
  return flags.sort((a, b) => b.lastAt - a.lastAt);
}
```

```js
/**
 * An admin takes credits off `fromId`: back to `toId` (RETURN) or out of the
 * economy (REMOVE, toId null). Never below zero: returns what was recovered.
 */
export function reverse({ adminId, fromId, toId = null, amount, note }) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    if (!Number.isInteger(amount) || amount < 1) throw new CreditError("Pick an amount");
    if (toId === fromId) throw new CreditError("Pick two different players");
    // Locked in a fixed order, so two reversals between the same pair can't deadlock.
    const locked = new Map();
    for (const id of [fromId, toId].filter(Boolean).sort()) locked.set(id, await lockAccount(client, id));
    const from = locked.get(fromId);
    const taken = Math.min(amount, from.balance);
    await setBalance(client, fromId, from.balance - taken);
    await ledger(client, { userId: fromId, kind: toId ? "admin_return_out" : "admin_remove", amount: -taken, balanceAfter: from.balance - taken, note, actorId: adminId });
    if (toId) {
      const to = locked.get(toId);
      await setBalance(client, toId, to.balance + taken);
      await ledger(client, { userId: toId, kind: "admin_return_in", amount: taken, balanceAfter: to.balance + taken, note, actorId: adminId });
    }
    return { recovered: taken, requested: amount };
  });
}

/** FREEZE / UNFREEZE: a frozen account can't sit at credit tables or claim. */
export function setFrozen({ adminId, userId, frozen, note }) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const a = await lockAccount(client, userId);
    await client.query("update credit_accounts set frozen_at = $2, frozen_note = $3 where user_id = $1", [userId, frozen ? new Date() : null, frozen ? note : null]);
    await ledger(client, { userId, kind: frozen ? "freeze" : "unfreeze", amount: 0, balanceAfter: a.balance, note, actorId: adminId });
    return { frozen: !!frozen };
  });
}

/** DISMISS: the pair's flag stays hidden until new credits flow between them. */
export function dismissFlag({ adminId, loserId, winnerId, note }) {
  return withTransaction(async (client) => {
    const a = await lockAccount(client, loserId);
    await client.query(
      `insert into credit_flag_reviews (loser_id, winner_id, reviewed_by, note) values ($1, $2, $3, $4)
       on conflict (loser_id, winner_id) do update set reviewed_at = now(), reviewed_by = $3, note = $4`,
      [loserId, winnerId, adminId, note],
    );
    await ledger(client, { userId: loserId, kind: "dismiss", amount: 0, balanceAfter: a.balance, note, actorId: adminId });
    return { dismissed: true };
  });
}
```

```js
// hands with everyone's cards, and one account's whole ledger.

import { pool } from "../db/index.js";
import { FLAG_WINDOW_DAYS, flagPairs, pairFlows } from "./flagRules.js";

const nameOf = (p) => (p?.username ? `${p.username} #${p.tag}` : "?");

/** Flagged pairs over the last 30 days, newest flow first. */
export async function flaggedPairs() {
  const { rows: hands } = await pool.query("select id, played_at, is_private, seats from credit_hands where played_at > now() - make_interval(days => $1)", [FLAG_WINDOW_DAYS]);
  const { rows: reviews } = await pool.query("select loser_id, winner_id, reviewed_at from credit_flag_reviews");
  const flags = flagPairs(
    pairFlows(hands.map((h) => ({ id: h.id, playedAt: h.played_at, isPrivate: h.is_private, seats: h.seats }))),
    new Map(reviews.map((r) => [`${r.loser_id}>${r.winner_id}`, r.reviewed_at])),
  );
  if (!flags.length) return [];
  const ids = [...new Set(flags.flatMap((f) => [f.loser, f.winner]))];
  const { rows: people } = await pool.query(
    `select p.id, p.username, p.tag, a.created_at, a.frozen_at,
            (select count(*)::int from credit_ledger l where l.user_id = p.id and l.kind = 'claim') as claims
       from profiles p left join credit_accounts a on a.user_id = p.id
      where p.id = any($1::uuid[])`,
    [ids],
  );
  const by = new Map(people.map((p) => [p.id, p]));
  return flags.map((f) => ({
    ...f,
    loserName: nameOf(by.get(f.loser)),
    winnerName: nameOf(by.get(f.winner)),
    loserSince: by.get(f.loser)?.created_at ?? null,
    loserClaims: by.get(f.loser)?.claims ?? 0,
    loserFrozen: !!by.get(f.loser)?.frozen_at,
    winnerFrozen: !!by.get(f.winner)?.frozen_at,
  }));
}

async function accountSummary(userId) {
  const {
    rows: [r],
  } = await pool.query(
    `select p.id, p.username, p.tag, a.balance, a.frozen_at, a.frozen_note, a.created_at,
            s.table_name, s.tier, s.stack + s.pending as on_table
       from profiles p
       left join credit_accounts a on a.user_id = p.id
       left join credit_seats s on s.user_id = p.id
      where p.id = $1`,
    [userId],
  );
  if (!r) return null;
  return {
    userId: r.id,
    name: nameOf(r),
    balance: Number(r.balance ?? 0),
    frozen: !!r.frozen_at,
    frozenNote: r.frozen_note,
    since: r.created_at,
    seat: r.table_name ? { tableName: r.table_name, tier: r.tier, onTable: Number(r.on_table) } : null,
  };
}

/** Every hand two players sat in together (newest first, up to 200), each seat named. */
export async function pairDetail(a, b) {
  const { rows } = await pool.query(
    `select id, played_at, tier, is_private, seats, board, events from credit_hands
      where seats @> $1::jsonb and seats @> $2::jsonb order by played_at desc limit 200`,
    [JSON.stringify([{ user_id: a }]), JSON.stringify([{ user_id: b }])],
  );
  const ids = [...new Set(rows.flatMap((h) => h.seats.map((s) => s.user_id)).filter(Boolean))];
  const { rows: people } = await pool.query("select id, username, tag from profiles where id = any($1::uuid[])", [ids]);
  const names = new Map(people.map((p) => [p.id, nameOf(p)]));
  return {
    hands: rows.map((h) => ({
      id: h.id,
      playedAt: h.played_at,
      tier: h.tier,
      isPrivate: h.is_private,
      board: h.board,
      events: h.events,
      seats: h.seats.map((s) => ({ ...s, name: names.get(s.user_id) ?? "?" })),
    })),
    accounts: [await accountSummary(a), await accountSummary(b)],
  };
}

/** "NAME#TAG" → the account, its seat, and its ledger (newest first, up to 500 rows). */
export async function accountLookup(q) {
  const match = /^\s*([^#\s]+)\s*#\s*([A-Za-z0-9]{4})\s*$/.exec(q);
  if (!match) throw new CreditError("Search as NAME#TAG");
  const {
    rows: [p],
  } = await pool.query("select id from profiles where lower(username) = lower($1) and tag = upper($2)", [match[1], match[2]]);
  if (!p) throw new CreditError("No such player");
  const { rows } = await pool.query(
    "select id, kind, amount, balance_after, tier, hand_id, note, created_at from credit_ledger where user_id = $1 order by id desc limit 500",
    [p.id],
  );
  return { ...(await accountSummary(p.id)), ledger: rows.map((r) => ({ ...r, amount: Number(r.amount), balance_after: Number(r.balance_after) })) };
}
```

```js
/** standUp(userId, reason) → resolves once that player is off any credit table with their credits home. */
export function adminRouter({ standUp = async () => {} } = {}) {
  const router = express.Router();
  router.use(requireAdmin, express.json({ limit: "8kb" }), requireDb);
  const handle = (fn) => async (req, res) => {
    try {
      res.json(await fn(req));
    } catch (err) {
      if (err instanceof CreditError) return res.status(400).json({ error: err.message });
      console.error("[admin]", err);
      res.status(500).json({ error: "Something went wrong" });
    }
  };

  router.get("/check", handle(() => economyCheck()));
  router.get("/flags", handle(() => flaggedPairs()));
  router.get("/pairs/:loser/:winner", handle((req) => pairDetail(idOf(req.params.loser), idOf(req.params.winner))));
  router.get("/accounts", handle((req) => accountLookup(String(req.query.q ?? ""))));
  router.post(
    "/reverse",
    handle(async (req) => {
      const note = noteOf(req.body);
      const fromId = idOf(req.body.from);
      const toId = req.body.to == null ? null : idOf(req.body.to);
      if (!Number.isInteger(req.body.amount) || req.body.amount < 1) throw new CreditError("Pick an amount");
      await standUp(fromId, "admin"); // their stack comes home first, so it can be taken back
      return accounts.reverse({ adminId: req.user.id, fromId, toId, amount: req.body.amount, note });
    }),
  );
  router.post(
    "/freeze",
    handle(async (req) => {
      const note = noteOf(req.body);
      const userId = idOf(req.body.userId);
      const result = await accounts.setFrozen({ adminId: req.user.id, userId, frozen: !!req.body.frozen, note });
      if (result.frozen) await standUp(userId, "frozen");
      return result;
    }),
  );
  router.post(
    "/dismiss",
    handle((req) => accounts.dismissFlag({ adminId: req.user.id, loserId: idOf(req.body.loserId), winnerId: idOf(req.body.winnerId), note: noteOf(req.body) })),
  );
  return router;
}
```

```js
// USE CREDITS — the signed-in player's credits from /api/credits/me: balance,
// CLAIM, whether they can play (and why not), rathole minimums by tier and a
// restart notice. Guests get null. enabled: false waits for refresh().

import { useCallback, useEffect, useState } from "react";
import { api } from "../lib/api";
import { useAuth } from "./useAuth";

const CREDITS = { base: "/api/credits" };

export function useCredits({ enabled = true } = {}) {
  const { isGuest, loading } = useAuth();
  const [credits, setCredits] = useState(null);
  const [error, setError] = useState("");
  const [busy, setBusy] = useState(false);

  const refresh = useCallback(async () => {
    if (isGuest) return setCredits(null);
    try {
      setCredits(await api("/me", CREDITS));
      setError("");
    } catch (err) {
      setError(err.message);
    }
  }, [isGuest]);

  useEffect(() => {
    if (enabled && !loading) refresh();
  }, [enabled, loading, refresh]);

  const claim = useCallback(async () => {
    setBusy(true);
    try {
      setCredits(await api("/claim", { method: "POST", ...CREDITS }));
      setError("");
    } catch (err) {
      setError(err.message);
    } finally {
      setBusy(false);
    }
  }, []);

  return { credits, error, busy, refresh, claim };
}
```

```jsx
// CLAIM — back up to 1,000 credits once a day, or why not yet.

import React, { useEffect, useState } from "react";
import { Btn } from "../PixelUI";
import { INK, TONES } from "../pixelTokens";
import { fmt } from "../../utils/poker/tiers";

/** 51_600_000 -> "14h 20m" (rounded up to the minute). */
export function untilText(ms) {
  const minutes = Math.max(0, Math.ceil(ms / 60_000));
  return `${Math.floor(minutes / 60)}h ${minutes % 60}m`;
}

/** claim: the server's claim state ({ state: "ready" | "wait" | "over" | "frozen", … }). */
export default function ClaimButton({ claim, onClaim, busy = false }) {
  const [now, setNow] = useState(() => Date.now());
  useEffect(() => {
    if (claim?.state !== "wait") return;
    const id = setInterval(() => setNow(Date.now()), 30_000);
    return () => clearInterval(id);
  }, [claim?.state]);

  if (!claim || claim.state === "frozen") return null;
  const due = claim.state === "wait" && Date.parse(claim.nextAt) <= now;
  if (claim.state === "ready" || due) {
    return (
      <Btn tone={TONES.poison} onClick={onClaim} disabled={busy} className="w-full">
        {claim.state === "ready" ? `Claim +${fmt(claim.amount)}` : "Claim"}
      </Btn>
    );
  }
  return (
    <div
      role="status"
      className="font-pixel-display text-[10px] uppercase text-center px-3 flex items-center justify-center"
      style={{ minHeight: "var(--ctl)", backgroundColor: INK, border: "3px solid #2a234d", color: "#c8b890" }}
    >
      {claim.state === "wait" ? `Next claim in ${untilText(Date.parse(claim.nextAt) - now)}` : "Over 1,000: nothing to claim"}
    </div>
  );
}
```

```markdown
## 8. Credit tables

Signed-in players with a linked Google or Discord login can also play for
**credits**, which stay with your account.

* **Your balance.** You start with 1000 credits. Once every 24 hours, if your
  balance plus what you have at a table is under 1000, CLAIM tops you back up

* **Stakes.** The host picks LOW (blinds 5/10, buy in 200–1000), MID

* **Buying in.** Sitting down moves the credits you choose from your balance

  must bring at least what you left with. You can sit at one credit table at
  a time.
* **The big blind.** A newcomer, or a player back from sitting out, is dealt
  in when the big blind reaches them, and posts it like everyone else.
* **Adding chips.** Between hands, or after you fold, ADD CHIPS tops your
  stack up to the table's maximum; the chips land before the next deal. Out
  of chips, REBUY works the same way from the table's minimum.
* **Leaving.** Your stack goes back to your balance. In a hand, your cards
  fold, unless you're all-in: then the hand plays out and whatever it wins
  goes to your balance.
* **Closing.** When the host closes the table, the hand in play finishes
  first, then everyone's credits go home. If the server restarts, every stack
  goes back to its balance and a hand that was being played doesn't count.
* **The leaderboard** ranks everyone who has played for credits by balance
```
