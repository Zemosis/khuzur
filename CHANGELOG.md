# What's new

Every version of Khuzur Card Hall, newest first. The site reads this file:
the newest version here is the one the main menu shows, and the PATCH NOTES
page lists them all. Write for players.

The hall is in early access, so versions are 0.x: the middle number moves
for a new game or a big new system, the last for everything else. 1.0.0 is
the first stable release.

## 0.6.0 — 28 October 2026

### Blackjack is here
- **The hall's fourth game.** Pick **BLACKJACK** on the main menu. You play against the dealer, not each other, at a casino half-moon table: five players sit just outside its rim, each with a betting circle on the felt in front of them.
- **Meet the Hooded House.** The dealer sits in a gold frame above the felt, a faceless figure in a purple hood with two gold eyes. It calls the round as it goes: PLACE YOUR BETS, the card it shows, its hole card, each card it draws, DEALER BUSTS.
- **Watch every card come out of the shoe.** A new shoe is shuffled on the felt, then the cards are dealt round one by one, flying out face down and turning over as they land. The dealer's hole card flips when it's revealed, and at the end of the round the cards are swept into the discard tray.
- **See what everyone does.** BET, READY, HIT, STAND, DOUBLE, SPLIT, BUST and BLACKJACK! pop up over each player as it happens. Chips drop into the circle as you bet. Results show only once the dealer's last card has landed: then lost bets go to the dealer and winnings fly onto your bet. An arrow bobs over the hand being played.
- **A scoreboard beside the table** ranks everyone by what they've won or lost since sitting down, with the rounds they've played and won, how often they've rebought, and their balance. A rebuy tops up your chips but your losses still count.
- **Free and credit tables.** Free tables seat you with 1,000 play chips, and **REBUY** tops you back up when you run low. Credit tables play for real credits and need a linked Google or Discord login, like the rest of credit play.
- **Play with friends or alone.** Host a public or private table for up to 5 players, or join one with its code or invite link. **PLAY ALONE** makes a private table and seats you in one click, and friends can still join with its code.
- **Bet from your balance, with no limit.** There's no buy-in and no table maximum: bet anything from 10 up to everything you have. Chip buttons from 10 to 5K, an exact-amount box, CLEAR, REBET, then **DEAL**.
- **Standard casino rules.** Blackjack pays 3 to 2, and the dealer stands on all 17s. When the dealer shows an Ace you can take **insurance**, which pays 2 to 1. **Double** on any first two cards, even after a split, and **split** any pair up to 4 hands. The rulebook has a Blackjack page with the rest.
- **Every round pays XP and gold,** like a poker hand: 4 XP and 3 gold, or 6 XP and 8 gold when you come out ahead, at most one round every 30 seconds. Playing alone counts.
- Miss 5 betting rounds in a row and you're stood up from the table. Leave in the middle of a round and your hands stand; at a credit table your winnings still reach your balance.

### Your Blackjack record
- **A Blackjack tab on your profile,** split into free and credits: rounds, win rate, blackjacks, biggest win and net result.
- **Each table you played at is a match** in your history. Its page shows your rounds in order, your hands next to the dealer's and how each one ended.
- **The leaderboard has a Blackjack column,** so every player's credits still add up game by game.

### Also
- **The CREDITS tab fits on one screen** in every game's lobby, like the FREE tab: your balance and CLAIM now share a row, and the panels only take the room they need.

## 0.5.2 — 27 October 2026

- **Link Google or Discord to your account.** Your profile has a new **Linked logins** list. Press **LINK** next to Google or Discord, approve it there, and it's added to the account you're signed in to, even if it uses a different email. After that you can sign in with your email and password or with the linked login, and keep your level, gold and stats either way.
- **Linking unlocks credit play.** Signed up with email and password? Link Google or Discord and you can play for credits. The CREDITS tab's message has a **LINK** button that takes you straight there.
- A Google or Discord account can be linked to one player only. You can **unlink** one as long as you keep another way to sign in.

## 0.5.1 — 26 October 2026

### The shop is open
- **SHOP** on the main menu opens the shop over the menu. Your gold is in its corner.
- **Avatar slots.** Keep more than one painted avatar: **slot 2** costs **1,500 gold** and **slot 3** costs **3,000** (buy slot 2 first). Buying takes two clicks, BUY then SPEND, so nothing goes by accident.
- **Paint and switch.** Once a slot is yours, PAINT IT opens the painter on that slot. Switch between your paintings from your player badge or your profile, and everyone at the table sees the one you wear.
- **Avatar storage in the painter** shows all three slots. Click an empty slot you own to save your drawing there. Click a locked one and the shop opens right over the painter; PAINT IT brings you back to that slot.
- **Buying looks like buying.** Coins fly out of your gold and drop into the slot, its padlock rattles, and the slot bursts open with an **UNLOCKED!** stamp and a chime. With animations set to REDUCED it just opens.
- **Coming soon:** avatar borders, titles and card variants. They're in the shop now, marked SOON.

### Gold is for playing people
- **Online games against CPUs alone no longer pay XP or gold.** They're the same as practice. A Thirteen or Muushig match pays only if at least two people (signed-in players or guests) play it to the end. A poker hand pays only if at least two people are dealt in.
- If you end up the only person at the table, the end of the match says why it paid nothing.
- Each rulebook's **XP, levels and gold** section says so.

## 0.5.0 — 26 October 2026

### XP, levels and gold
- **Coins are now gold.** Same balance, new name. Gold is for the shop (coming soon), and it isn't credits.
- **See what you earned.** The end of every online Thirteen and Muushig match shows the XP and gold it paid you, your XP bar filling up, and **LEVEL UP!** when you reach a new level.
- **What a match pays:** the winner gets 70 XP and 80 gold, last place 30 XP and 20 gold, and the places between are spread evenly. Free and credit matches pay the same.
- **Poker pays too.** Each hand you're dealt into pays 4 XP and 3 gold (6 XP and 8 gold if you win it), at most one hand every 30 seconds.
- **New level curve.** Early levels come quickly; each level takes 50 XP more than the last, up to 1,500. Everyone keeps the level they had.
- **Level-ups pay gold:** 25 × the new level, doubled on every 5th (level 5 pays 250, level 10 pays 500).
- **Leaving early earns nothing.** If you leave a match and don't come back, the CPU playing your seat earns nothing for you. Come back before the end and you're paid in full.
- **Your level, XP and gold on the main menu.** Your player badge shows your level, and your gold (the coin) beside your credits. The gold rolls up after a match. Your leaderboard rank shows there only while you're in the top 500. The lobby's Adventurer card shows your gold too, and your profile shows your XP as a bar filling toward the next level.
- **Everyone's level at the table.** Signed-in players have a gold **LV** tag beside their name at Thirteen, Muushig and Poker tables, at the waiting table, and on match pages.
- Each rulebook has a new **XP, levels and gold** section.

### A tidier main menu
- **The top bar has two sides.** Rules, Ranks and Shop sit by the Khuzur logo on the left; your claim, your player badge and Settings sit on the right.
- **CLAIM is a small button now,** beside your badge instead of a strip across the top. When you're at 1,000 or more it's a dim, locked CLAIM (hidden on phones); while you wait it counts down.
- The "Card Hall" line and the tagline above the games are gone. It's just **CHOOSE YOUR GAME**.

### Cleaner profile stats
- **Credit figures only under CREDITS.** The ALL view no longer repeats your credit games in their own row. Under CREDITS you get what credit play came to: credits won or lost, per game, pots won and your biggest pot (no more "Credit games" beside "Games").
- **Overall no longer has a Poker section.** Poker's figures live on the POKER tab.

## 0.4.0 — 21 October 2026

### Muushig and Thirteen for credits
- Thirteen's and Muushig's lobbies have a **CREDITS** tab. Both are played for a stake: everyone at the table puts in the same amount, and **the match winner takes the pot**.
- Three stakes: **LOW 100**, **MID 500** and **HIGH 2,000**. The host picks one. Your stake leaves your balance when you sit, and comes back if you get up before the deal.
- Start with 2 or more players.
  - **Thirteen** is for people only: 2 to 4 players, and empty seats stay empty.
  - **Muushig** always seats 5, so **Hard bots** fill the empty seats. Bots put nothing in, and **if a bot wins, the pot is lost**.
- Leave mid-match and a Hard bot plays your seat. Come back within **2 rounds** (use the table code, or **RETURN** on the CREDITS tab) and the seat is yours again. Stay away longer and the seat is the bot's.
- When the match ends, **PLAY AGAIN** stakes you for the next one.
- Thirteen and Muushig wins and losses show on the leaderboard's breakdown, and a credit match's page shows what it was played for.
- Both rulebooks have a new **Playing for credits** section.

### Profiles and match pages
- Your Thirteen, Muushig and Overall stats have an **All · Free · Credits** switch. Credits shows your credit matches: credits won or lost, pots won and your biggest pot. Credit matches in **Recent matches** show what you won or lost.
- **No more rating.** Credits are the ranked play, so the hidden rating is gone and match pages no longer show a rating number next to each player. A credit match's page shows each player's credits won or lost there instead.

## 0.3.0 — 20 October 2026

### Match pages
- Open any online Thirteen or Muushig match from **Recent matches** on a profile: the final standings, one player's summary against the table, the score over the rounds, and each round's results and key plays.
- Pick a player in the standings to see the match from their side. Public matches can be shared with a link.

### Smarter CPUs in every game
- **Thirteen.** Medium plans its whole hand, keeps its aces and 2s while its hand is big, and stops a player about to go out. Hard also counts every card played, reads who passed, and plays each move out in its head before choosing.
- **Muushig.** Every level judges its hand far better: a dealer whose face-up trump is the ace always goes in and takes it, a pile in the bag. Medium counts the cards played and fights for its first pile. Hard plays each choice out against hundreds of deals of the cards it can't see, and plays to win the match, not just the round.
- **Poker.** Medium respects the size of a raise and the bets it faces. Hard reads hands from how each player bets, remembers how everyone at the table plays (a raise from someone who raises everything says little), bluffs where a bluff works, and plays all-in or fold when its stack is short.
- Easy is still the friendly table in every game, just a little less clumsy.

### Main menu and profiles
- A **RANKS** button opens the leaderboard, and your rank shows next to your credits.
- The daily **CLAIM** now sits on the main menu, with a countdown to the next one.
- The leaderboard shows where each player's credits came from: claims and each game's wins and losses.
- Poker profiles show how often you've shown each hand at a showdown, and how often it won.

### At the table
- Poker tables have a real deck and muck on the felt: the deck shuffles, deals and slides back, and folded hands land on the muck.
- Chat: turn speech bubbles off in Settings, or mute a player just for yourself.
- Thirteen's menu tile now says what it plays: 2 to 4 players.
